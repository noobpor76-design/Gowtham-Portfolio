Projects

SuperMathWorksheets

Full-stack educational web application

Visit supermathworksheets.com

A free printable worksheet platform for preschool and kindergarten teachers and parents. Visitors browse by category, preview a worksheet in a modal, and download the PDF. Content is organised as Category, Topic and Worksheet, and is published through Django admin workflows.

  • Dynamic worksheet category filtering and dynamically rendered pages
  • Responsive preview modals
  • Free PDF downloads for every worksheet
  • PostgreSQL-backed content library organised by category and topic
  • Cloudflare R2 storage for 2,000+ images and PDFs
  • 95+ pages built on reusable Django templates
